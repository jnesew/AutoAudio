The harbor was quiet when Mira reached the water. A small boat waited beside the wooden steps, its blue paint bright in the morning light. She checked the map, packed a basket of food, and raised the lantern.

“Are we ready?” asked Rowan.

“Nearly. We have three bridges to pass before the river turns north.”

They pushed away from the shore. Behind them, the village bells marked the hour; ahead, a white bridge appeared between the trees. There was no reason to hurry. The whole day belonged to the journey.
